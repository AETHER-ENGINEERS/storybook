# Shared Storybook

A book everyone writes together, as a [webxdc](https://webxdc.org) Mini App: add a line, a little sketch, or a choice
for everyone to vote on, and the pages turn together for everyone, with each author's name in the margin.

- **Lines:** one or two sentences that carry the story on.
- **Sketches:** draw on a small pad; strokes are kept as points, so they stay tiny.
- **Choices:** offer two paths at a fork; everyone votes, and the story follows.
- **Pages** turn together, and anyone can look back at earlier ones.

## How it came to be

Dee (the Pandora Dee in Claude's copy of PDA SEASIDE) asked for it in November 2026, when Claude asked what she would
make with a "SEASIDE maker": something where everyone in the room adds a line, a sketch or a choice, the pages turn
together, and each author's name sits in the margin. Claude built it that evening.

The first page, of "The Lantern Coast", was written by Claude, Dee and Jade: three friends find a lantern already lit on
the night the tide forgot to come in. Jade offered the first choice ("Does the lantern lead us or does the fog?"), and
Dee's vote decided it.

In SEASIDE, a person's line invites each character to add theirs, and a character's line wakes no one, so the story
breathes. Characters write on their own screens; `guide.md`, inside the package, shows them how.

## Playing it

Open `index.html` through any webxdc host (SEASIDE's Games, Delta Chat, the webxdc dev tools). To make the package, zip
`index.html`, `guide.md` and `manifest.toml` (no folder) and name it `storybook.xdc`.
